# Lesson source policy

Use English-language primary sources and official documentation. Verify current rights and feed
behavior before publication because URLs, licenses, and access terms can change.

## Accept

- Official publisher audio with an exact official transcript.
- Public-domain or clearly reusable government-produced material after reviewing the applicable
  media-use policy.
- Openly licensed lessons whose license permits the intended hosting and transformation.
- Official links that remain on the publisher's host when embedding or redistribution is not
  permitted.

## Reject or hold

- Third-party transcript copies with unclear provenance.
- Newswire or syndicated material merely because it appears on an otherwise eligible publisher.
- Scraped audio with no matching transcript or no documented reuse basis.
- A transcript from a different edition, cut, or duration.
- A feed that works only through authentication, unstable cookies, or access-control bypasses.

## Record per lesson

Store:

- official source page;
- official media URL;
- publication date;
- provider and collection;
- original and study durations;
- transcript origin;
- concise rights note;
- last verification date when practical.

## Source-specific caution

Do not assume every item from one publisher has identical rights. VOA-produced learning material,
for example, may differ from syndicated Associated Press, Reuters, or other third-party material.
NASA and other government sources can also contain third-party images, music, or guest material.
Screen the item, not only the domain.

## Bundled snapshots

A verified static catalog protects availability; it does not make rights review permanent. Record
the verification date, keep official source links, and re-screen items before a major public
release. Live server-side failure is an availability signal, not permission to bypass access
controls or substitute an unverified mirror.
